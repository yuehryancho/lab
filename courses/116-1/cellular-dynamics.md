## Course description

Cells are never still. They crawl, divide, change shape, send and receive signals, and decide when to die. This course covers the moving parts of the cell: the cytoskeleton and motors that give cells shape and movement, the signaling networks that let cells sense nutrients, hormones, and stress, the machinery that drives the cell cycle and cytokinesis, the pathways of programmed cell death, and the adhesions and forces that hold cells together in tissues. Examples come from animal, yeast, and plant cells, with links to nutrition, disease, and food biotechnology throughout.

Together with Organelle Biology (year 2, spring), which covers the cell's compartments, this course gives you a complete picture of how a eukaryotic cell works.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the course, you will be able to:

1. Describe the assembly dynamics of actin filaments, microtubules, and intermediate filaments, and explain how motor proteins generate force and movement.
2. Explain the general principles of cell signaling and trace the major pathways: G protein-coupled receptors, receptor kinases, calcium, MAPK, PI3K–AKT–mTOR, and plant hormone perception.
3. Outline the cell cycle control system, the mechanics of mitosis and cytokinesis, and how animal and plant cells differ in dividing.
4. Compare the main forms of programmed cell death and give examples from development, immunity, and disease.
5. Explain how cells adhere, communicate, and sense force in tissues, including plasmodesmata and the plant cell wall.
6. Read and quantify a time-lapse microscopy movie and interpret a signaling or cell cycle experiment from the primary literature.

## Prerequisites

General Biology (b)(1)(2). Plant Biology is helpful. Students take Biochemistry (b)(1) concurrently; the course does not assume prior biochemistry.

## 設計理由與備課備忘（中文，自用）

### 定位與命名
- 大二上，2 學分。學程課程架構的選修群組已有 Cell Biology 與 Molecular and Cell Biology，依「有開就不要重複」的原則，這門課用 Cellular Dynamics 這個名稱，內容取細胞生物學裡「會動的那一半」：細胞骨架、運動、訊息傳遞、細胞週期、細胞死亡、黏著與力學。胞器生物學（大二下）講「隔間的那一半」。兩門合起來是一套完整的細胞生物學，但沒有一門叫 Cell Biology。
- 教科書和胞器生物學同一本 Essential Cell Biology，學生只買一次；這門用第 16、17、18、20 章，胞器用第 11、12、14、15、17 章，第 17 章細胞骨架兩門各講一半（這門講動態與運動，胞器講胞器定位）。
- 2026-10-24 定案課名；時段待定。

### 進度安排的考量
- 上學期行事曆未公布，先用週次；之後填日期與停課週。
- 順序：骨架 → 運動 → 訊息傳遞三週（期中前後各一半）→ 週期 → 死亡 → 組織與力學 → 應用。訊息傳遞的第 7 週（mTOR／AMPK、植物 TOR）是食品營養那一半學生最有感的一週，放在期中前。
- 第 9 週植物訊息傳遞可改寫研究所植物生理與逆境反應的荷爾蒙與訊息週；第 12 週細胞死亡與第 7 週壓力訊號可借用蛋白質恆定概論的材料。
- 第 11 週的 Fiji 影片分析作業是這門課和別人最不一樣的地方：用公開的細胞分裂縮時影片，學生量測有絲分裂時間或細胞移動速度。需要先準備影片、Fiji 安裝說明與一份範例分析，備課量比一般問題集高，但可年年重用。

### 大一下→大二上→大二下的銜接
- 大一下植物生物學給整株植物；大二上這門給細胞的動態與訊號；大二下胞器生物學給隔間；之後大三下植物脂質、大四上蛋白質恆定概論。第一屆（116-1 的大二）修過大一下的植物生物學，銜接完整。
