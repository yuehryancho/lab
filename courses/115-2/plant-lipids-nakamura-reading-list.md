# Plant Lipids: Reading list built on the Nakamura lab publications

> 給自己的說明（2026-10-24）：這份清單把 Yuki Nakamura（RIKEN CSRS 植物脂質研究團隊，前中研院植微所）的發表依 Plant Lipids 的週次整合。
> 這個環境無法連 PubMed、Europe PMC、Google Scholar 與 RIKEN 網站，所以清單只涵蓋我能確認或有把握的約 50 篇，不是他全部約 90 篇著作。
> 每條標記：**[V]** 本次由搜尋結果或你的 CV 確認過書目；**[M]** 出自我的記憶，書目細節請在上線前核對。
> 補齊其餘著作請用：[Google Scholar 個人頁](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=AvoaipUAAAAJ&hl=en)、[RIKEN Plant Lipid Research Team](https://www.riken.jp/en/research/labs/csrs/plant_lipid_res/index.html)、PubMed 查詢 `Nakamura Y[au] AND (Academia Sinica[ad] OR RIKEN[ad]) AND lipid`。
> 尚未對應到 Nakamura 論文的週次（第 2、7、11 週）維持原本的教科書與回顧文獻。

## Wk 14 · Lipid analysis and data-reading workshop

- Dataset sources for the workshop: the lipidomic tables in Nakamura et al. *New Phytol* 203 (2014) and Nguyen & Nakamura *Plant Cell* 35 (2023); the PNAS 2009 lipin paper for thin-layer chromatography and fatty acid profiling by GC.

## Wk 15 · Plant oils for food, nutrition, and industry

- **R** [V] Tan Y-R, Nakamura Y. (2022) *J Exp Bot*: a metabolic route from phosphatidylethanolamine to seed oil; overexpression raised seed triacylglycerol by about 20% per dry weight (figure from the RIKEN press release; confirm against the paper).
- **S** [M] Angkawijaya et al. (2017) *Plant Cell Environ*: engineering phospholipid synthesis to sustain root growth under low phosphate.
- **S** [V] Hung et al. (2013, 2016): Chlamydomonas triacylglycerol as an algal oil platform.
